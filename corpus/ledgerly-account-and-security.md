# Ledgerly: account and security

## Reset your password

On the sign-in page, click "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. Ledgerly sends a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or request a new link; each new link cancels the previous one.

If you are already signed in, you can change your password in Settings > Security > Change password. You will need your current password.

## Two-factor authentication

Ledgerly supports two-factor authentication (2FA) with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password. Turn it on in Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication and scan the QR code. Save the ten backup codes shown at the end: each code works once and lets you sign in if you lose your phone.

Team plan owners can require 2FA for every member of the workspace.

## Lost access to 2FA

If you lost both your phone and your backup codes, contact support from the email address of your account. For security, support verifies your identity with details of your last invoices before turning 2FA off. This usually takes one business day.

## Team roles

On the Team plan there are three roles. Owners manage billing and members. Admins can do everything except billing. Members can create and send invoices but cannot delete clients or change settings.

## Deleting your account

The account owner can delete the workspace in Settings > Danger zone. Deletion is permanent after 30 days; during those 30 days you can restore the workspace by signing in.
