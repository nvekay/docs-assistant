# Ledgerly: plans, billing and exports

## Plans

Ledgerly has three plans:

- Free: up to 5 clients and 10 invoices per month.
- Pro: $12 per month, unlimited clients and invoices, recurring invoices and payment reminders.
- Team: $29 per month for up to 5 users, with roles and a shared client list.

Annual billing gives two months free. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time in Settings > Plan; the change is prorated on your next bill.

## Your Ledgerly bill

Ledgerly charges your card on the same day each month. Receipts are emailed to the account owner and can be downloaded from Settings > Billing history. To change the card, open Settings > Payment method.

If a payment fails, Ledgerly retries it after 3 and 7 days and emails the account owner each time. After 14 days without payment the workspace switches to read-only mode: you can still view and download invoices, but you cannot create or send new ones.

## Exporting invoices

You can export invoices to CSV or to a ZIP archive of PDFs. Open Invoices, filter by date range or status, and click "Export". CSV exports include the invoice number, client, issue date, due date, currency, subtotal, tax, total and status. Exports are available on all plans, including Free.

## Cancelling

To cancel, open Settings > Plan and click "Cancel subscription". Your paid features stay active until the end of the current billing period. Your data is kept for 90 days after cancellation, so you can export it or reactivate the account.
