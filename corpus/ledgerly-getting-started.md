# Ledgerly: getting started

Ledgerly is an invoicing app for freelancers and small businesses. You create clients, send invoices, and track who has paid.

## Create your workspace

Sign up at the Ledgerly website with your email address. After confirming your email, you are asked for your business name, country and default currency. The default currency can be changed later in Settings > Business profile, but invoices that were already sent keep their original currency.

## Add your first client

Open Clients and click "New client". Only the client name and email are required. You can also add a billing address, a tax ID and a default payment term (for example, 14 days). Clients can be imported from a CSV file with the columns name, email, address and tax_id.

## Send your first invoice

Open Invoices and click "New invoice". Choose a client, add line items with a description, quantity and unit price, and Ledgerly calculates taxes and the total. Click "Send" to email the invoice as a PDF with a payment link. The invoice status changes from Draft to Sent, then to Viewed when the client opens it, and to Paid when payment arrives.

## Recurring invoices

For retainers and subscriptions, open an invoice and choose "Make recurring". You can repeat it weekly, monthly or yearly. Ledgerly creates and sends each new invoice automatically on the scheduled date, using the same line items.
